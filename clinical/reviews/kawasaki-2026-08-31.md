## Findings

No care-changing discrepancy requiring a production correction was identified.

The module was concordant with the 2024 AHA statement on the major resident-facing decision points reviewed, including:

- complete KD diagnosis with at least 4 principal features by illness day 4, with experienced clinicians able to diagnose earlier in appropriate cases;
- the incomplete-KD inflammation gate, supportive laboratory criteria, and positive-echocardiogram pathway;
- IVIG 2 g/kg as initial therapy, moderate-dose aspirin framing, and IVIG resistance defined by persistent or recrudescent fever at least 36 hours after completion of the IVIG infusion;
- high-risk emphasis on age under 6 months and baseline proximal RCA/LAD Z score at least 2.5;
- the 2024 imaging update allowing selected patients with normal baseline and 1- to 2-week studies and resolved inflammation to omit routine 4- to 6-week imaging, while patients with coronary Z score at least 2.5 require substantially more frequent acute surveillance;
- maximal-ever coronary involvement remaining central to long-term risk classification, including formerly giant aneurysms;
- contemporary adjunctive/refractory treatment options and explicit specialist-led framing where comparative efficacy evidence remains limited.

A current-guidance search through 2026-08-31 identified no later U.S. Kawasaki disease management guideline superseding the 2024 AHA scientific statement. The May 2026 AHA science advisory addresses international collaboration and access rather than replacing the treatment recommendations. The February 2026 Canadian Paediatric Society practice point remains a useful operational cross-check.
