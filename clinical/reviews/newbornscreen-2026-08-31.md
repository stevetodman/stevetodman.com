## Scope reviewed

- 2025 AAP pulse-oximetry screening algorithm and thresholds
- preductal/postductal measurement sites
- repeat-screen sequence
- routine screening timing and NICU/supplemental-oxygen exceptions
- failed-screen response and non-CCHD hypoxemia differential
- limitations, especially false reassurance for lesions without early hypoxemia
- resident quiz and take-home summary

## Findings

The production module’s core algorithm was consistent with current AAP/CDC guidance: right-hand plus foot measurements; immediate failure for any saturation below 90%; pass only when both sites are at least 95% with an absolute difference of 3% or less; one repeat after an indeterminate screen; and evaluation for both CCHD and non-CCHD causes after a failed screen.

One clinically meaningful teaching gap was identified: routine timing and the NICU/supplemental-oxygen exception were not stated explicitly. The reviewed guidance specifies routine screening at 24 hours of age or later (or as late as possible before earlier discharge). In the NICU, screening is performed after supplemental oxygen has been weaned; if room-air weaning before discharge is not possible, the AAP report states that echocardiography is warranted and pulse-oximetry screening is unnecessary unless required by state law.

The module is being updated to state this timing/special-circumstance nuance while preserving the existing instruction to follow institutional and state requirements.
