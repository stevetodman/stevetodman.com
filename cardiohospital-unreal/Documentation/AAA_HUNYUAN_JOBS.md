# Hunyuan job cards — Exam Room props

Use these only after the Blender architectural shell exists. One object per job. Neutral ground. No people, logos, charts, or room.

Hosted Hunyuan3D 3.1 is preferred when 8-view + 4K PBR is available. Local Hunyuan3D 2.1 is acceptable. Export GLB into `SourceAssets/ExamRoom/Props/<Asset>/hunyuan/`.

## HY-01 Pediatric exam table

- Envelope: 1.82 × 0.72 × 0.94 m, pivot at floor center, head toward +X
- Prompt: Original unbranded pediatric exam table, three-section teal medical vinyl mattress, warm-white powder-coated cabinet base, one pull-out step, paper-roll at the head, brushed-steel handles, real-world scale, studio lighting, no logos
- Accept if: length 1.73–1.91 m, width 0.68–0.76 m, surface height 0.89–0.99 m; separate step and paper-roll; no fused floor
- Prefer reworking the PR #22 mesh if it already meets this

## HY-02 Parent chair

## HY-04 ECG cart

- Envelope: 0.45 × 0.80 × 0.80 m parked against the east wall

- Accept if: display is a blank dark glass, no lead-wire spaghetti fused to the cart, depth ≤ 0.50 m

## HY-05 Wall blood-pressure housing

- Envelope: 0.20 × 0.20 × 1.10 m keep-clear, device itself ~0.12 m deep
- Prompt: Wall-mounted analog sphygmomanometer housing, pale clinical plastic, round gauge, coiled tubing, empty cuff basket, unbranded, real-world scale
- Accept if: no brand type, no mercury column, mounts to a wall with a flat back

## Shared negatives (every job)

No GE, Philips, Welch Allyn, Midmark, Stryker, or hospital name. No faces. No paper with text. No floor plane in the mesh. No toy colors.
