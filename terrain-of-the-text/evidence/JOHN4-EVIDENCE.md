# John 4 Evidence Note — Round 0

## Question

What may the atlas safely say about the route through Samaria, Sychar, and the force of John 4:4?

## Route through Samaria

Josephus states that it was the custom of Galileans traveling to Jerusalem for festivals to pass through Samaritan territory. This is direct evidence against the popular blanket claim that Jewish travelers normally avoided Samaria.

Primary-source access:
- Josephus, *Antiquities* 20, chapter 6: https://penelope.uchicago.edu/josephus/ant-20.html
- Parallel accessible text: https://www.ccel.org/ccel/josephus/complete.ii.xxi.vi.html

### Allowed atlas claim

The Samaritan route was a normal, direct route used by Galileans in at least some important travel contexts. Jewish-Samaritan hostility was real and could make travel through the region socially charged or dangerous, but the map must not imply that Jesus took a road Jews ordinarily refused to use.

### Interpretive boundary

John's wording that Jesus `had to pass through Samaria` may carry narrative or theological significance, but geography alone does not establish that conclusion. The atlas should label such a reading as interpretation.

## Jewish-Samaritan hostility

Josephus reports violent conflict involving Galilean pilgrims and Samaritans on the route. Elsewhere he reports Samaritans secretly placing dead human remains in the Jerusalem temple precincts during Passover.

This supports a strong claim about hostility and social tension, not a universal rule about route avoidance.

## Sychar

OpenBible.info currently aggregates two principal identifications:

1. **Askar** — presented by that aggregation as the leading candidate.
   - representative coordinate: 32.217760, 35.288970
   - coordinate source named there: Digital Archaeological Atlas of the Holy Land
2. **Sychar as another name for Shechem / Tell Balatah** — a serious alternate identification.
   - Tell Balatah representative coordinate: 32.213611, 35.281944

Sources:
- https://www.openbible.info/geo/ancient/a27b472/sychar
- https://www.openbible.info/geo/modern/mb04b16/askar
- https://www.openbible.info/geo/modern/m657590/tell-balatah

OpenBible's numerical confidence values are **not** adopted as probabilities in this atlas. The project itself describes them as relative indicators based on its consulted sources rather than exhaustive scholarship.

## Round 0 disposition

- `Sychar` remains **contested**.
- `Askar` and `Tell Balatah/Shechem` are both retained as candidate records.
- No probability weights will be displayed.
- The route-through-Samaria claim is corrected: do not teach normal avoidance.
- Round 2 must revisit Sychar with specialist literature before final episode copy is approved.
