# Contested Claims Ledger

Only claims whose uncertainty materially affects the map or interpretation belong here. Ordinary sourced facts do not need IDs.

## John 4 — force of `had to pass through Samaria`

**Status:** contested interpretive claim.

**Geographic facts allowed:** the Samaritan route is geographically direct; alternate routes existed; Jewish-Samaritan hostility is historically well attested.

**Not established by geography:** that Jesus used an abnormal route, or that `had to` must mean theological necessity.

**Display rule:** separate geographic facts from interpretive proposals.

## Sychar identification

**Status:** contested place identification.

**Action before mapping:** evidence review must identify the serious candidate sites, coordinates, arguments, and source quality. Do not preassign probability weights.

## Gerasene/Gadarene/Gergesene location

**Status:** contested textual/geographic identification.

**Action before mapping:** review Synoptic textual forms, candidate cities/territories, shoreline topography, and the distinction between city name and territorial designation.

## Exodus route and Sinai/Horeb identifications

**Status:** contested reconstruction.

**Action before mapping:** retain named serious reconstructions supported by the evidence review. Do not force the data into a fixed number of routes or imply archaeological consensus where none exists.
