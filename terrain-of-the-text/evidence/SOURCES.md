|---|---|---|---|
| Pleiades | ancient places, coordinates, identifiers | strong reference for Greco-Roman geography | verify attribution requirements per dataset version |

| SRTM or equivalent elevation data | terrain/elevation | physical geography | verify dataset/source terms |
| Primary literary sources | ancient travel/context claims | primary evidence | cite passage/work directly where used |
| Specialist archaeology/historical geography | disputed sites and routes | interpretive evidence | record source-specific arguments |

## Rule

A source being open, popular, or aggregated does not make every claim derived from it secure. Dataset provenance and scholarly identification are separate questions.
