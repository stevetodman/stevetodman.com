Interpretation boundary

  • Neither KD nor MIS-C has a single definitive diagnostic test.
  • Published group differences do not establish an individual patient diagnosis.
  • A finding associated with one syndrome does not rule out the other.
  • Unknown findings remain unknown and are never treated as normal.
  • Alternative infectious, inflammatory, toxic, and rheumatologic diagnoses still require clinical evaluation.
