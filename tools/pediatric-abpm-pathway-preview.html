Pediatric ABPM Pathway

A transparent, clinician-facing comparison of a complete ambulatory BP report with a confirmed office-BP context.

TEST PREVIEW — not for clinical use.
Use synthetic or de-identified values only. This browser-local prototype does not validate a monitor, establish a diagnosis, provide emergency triage, or select treatment.
What this test tool does—and does not do

It compares the six ABPM report means against the AHA 2022 pediatric thresholds, checks the quality metadata you attest to, and only then maps a confirmed office-BP context to a phenotype.

  • It accepts a report summary—not raw monitor files, PDFs, images, home BP, cuffless readings, or a fixed nighttime clock window.
  • For children under 13, it requires the validated report’s Wühl-based 95th-percentile limits, the height used, and the reference dataset. It never estimates or extrapolates them.
  • BP load is deliberately excluded. Dipping is displayed as a descriptive calculation only and never changes the phenotype.
  • Outpatient ages 5 to <18 only. Pregnancy, inpatient/acute settings, and active antihypertensive treatment require clinician-led interpretation outside this prototype.

Step 1 · Scope

Patient context

Age is calculated on the ABPM study date. This page does not transmit or retain entries; do not enter identifiers.

Step 2 · Quality gate

Confirm the complete ABPM report

AHA 2022 quality conditions are checked before an automated phenotype. A report-summary entry cannot independently audit raw readings.

Important: ≥70% successful readings, sleep capture, and ≥1 reading/hour including sleep are required here. A 24-hour study is ideal; 18–20 hours may be acceptable if sleep is captured. BP load is not entered or used.
Step 3 · Office-BP bridge

Confirm the office-BP status

ABPM means alone cannot distinguish normotension, white-coat, masked, and ambulatory hypertension. This is a phenotype screen, not an office-BP calculator—use the separate office-BP test pathway when an AAP office-BP assessment is needed.

Enter date of birth and ABPM study date to display the correct office-BP bridge.
Step 4 · Report means and thresholds

Enter the six report means

Enter the unrounded report values in mm Hg where available. Classification checks every period and component; a single value meeting or exceeding its threshold is abnormal.

Algorithm, sources, and release boundary

Version: AHA-2022-pediatric-ABPM-summary-v1.0. This test route is clinician-facing decision support, not an autonomous diagnosis system.

  • AHA 2022 pediatric ABPM scientific statement: classification, quality conditions, diary-derived periods, and removal of BP load.
  • AAP 2017 pediatric office-BP guideline: office-BP pathway context.
  • Before any real clinical release: pediatric hypertension/nephrology review, blinded fixture comparison, privacy/security review, and regulatory review—especially before any raw monitor-file feature.

No data leaves this page. Do not use a public test route for patient identifiers.
