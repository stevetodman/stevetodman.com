2 · Report quality

Confirm the report once, audit details only when needed

This page applies a local automated-phenotype safety gate to AHA quality criteria. A suboptimal study may still contain clinically useful information and needs clinician review.

Fast lane for a complete standard report

Use only after you verify all seven required criteria in the complete report: device validation, cuff/arm, sleep, diary periods, hourly readings, application check, and exclusions.

Individual quality evidence

Choose confirmed, not documented, or not met. Missing documentation produces review required; it does not become a failed criterion or a pass.

24 hours is optimal; 18–20 hours can be acceptable if sleep is captured. This page uses mm Hg only and does not accept BP load.
